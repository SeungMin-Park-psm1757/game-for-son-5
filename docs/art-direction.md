# Art Direction

## Shared Tone
- Warm family sports festival, not harsh competition.
- Soft paper-like panels over clean outdoor colors.
- Keep the center aiming lane visually calm and uncluttered.

## Home
- One scenic background, one information deck, one dock menu.
- Family support stays photo-led and compact.
- Decorative shapes should never overlap the main menu cards on mobile.

## Match
- Foreground detail budget goes to the bow, string, hands, and arrow nock.
- Background budget stays minimal: one frontal structure, two side frames, one distant layer.
- Top HUD only shows mode, arrow count, score, X, and level.
- Wind and release timing live in the lower coach card.

## Story
- Story scenes reuse the same sky, field, and paper-card palette as home and match.
- Portraits stay grounded in the family photo assets instead of full 3D characters.
- Accent colors should come from the active speaker portrait.

## Feedback
- Normal hit, 10, X, and overlap each get a different short briefing tone.
- Keep impact feedback readable within about 1.2 seconds.
- Prefer small motion and contrast shifts over heavy particles.
